Fast aviation calculations

Work through crosswind, wind triangle, pressure and density altitude, airspeed, time/speed/distance, fuel, descent and other common pilot calculations.

Aircraft-aware planning

Supported calculations expose inputs, formulas, assumptions and intermediate values so pilots can understand how results were produced.
