CXC Prep Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CXC Prep, provided by GF Productions, handles information when you use the CXC Prep application.

Information we collect

CXC Prep may collect information you provide when creating and using an account, including your email address, display name, selected country, selected syllabus, optional exam date, and other onboarding information you choose to provide.

The app may also store learning and usage information such as practice attempts, answers, scores, topic mastery, study progress, streaks, exam-simulation activity, and related educational activity associated with your account.

AI tutor information

If you use the AI tutor, your tutor messages and relevant learning context may be processed to provide a response. This may include information such as your question, selected topic, recent learning results, topic mastery, or other context needed to provide the tutoring feature. CXC Prep may use third-party AI service providers, including Google Gemini, to process AI tutor requests.

AI-generated responses may be inaccurate and should be checked against trusted learning materials.

Subscriptions and purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. CXC Prep does not receive your full payment-card details. The app may receive and store purchase or entitlement information needed to determine whether Premium features should be available to your account.

How we use information

Information may be used to operate the app, authenticate accounts, save progress, personalize study features, provide AI tutor responses, manage Premium access, troubleshoot problems, improve reliability, respond to support requests, and protect the service from misuse.

Service providers

CXC Prep may rely on third-party service providers to operate parts of the service. These may include Supabase for account, database, and backend functionality; Apple for App Store purchases and subscription processing; and Google Gemini for AI tutor functionality. Information is shared with these providers only as needed to provide the relevant feature, subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Data retention

We retain account and learning information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain your account, meet legal or security obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the reason it is stored.

Your choices and deletion requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your CXC Prep account. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. Some information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or transaction records.

Children and younger users

CXC Prep is an educational app that may be used by younger learners. Parents or guardians should supervise use where appropriate. If a parent or guardian believes that personal information has been provided inappropriately, they may contact us to request review or deletion.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

International processing

Depending on the service providers used to operate CXC Prep, information may be processed in countries other than the country in which you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app, service providers, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
